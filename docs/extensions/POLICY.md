# Extension policy

The app runs extensions that anyone publishes in their own repository. Nobody on the app's side hosts,
reviews, tests or recommends them. An extension is the work and the responsibility of its author, who
answers for following the law and the terms of the services it uses. Installing one is the user's choice.

The store lists public repositories that carry the extension topic. Being listed says nothing about an
extension's quality, legality or safety. The default order is by most recent update. Ratings are the
opinions of other users.

## What gets an extension blocked

We do not look for problems, but we act on reports of an extension that:

1. **Is malware**: it hides what it does, runs code loaded from elsewhere, or damages the user's device,
   network or accounts.
2. **Steals data**: it sends passwords, tokens, cookies or personal data anywhere the user was not told.
3. **Uses other people's accounts**: shared, stolen or scraped logins or tokens.
4. **Impersonates**: it presents itself as made or endorsed by someone who did not make it.

## Legal notices

We block an extension from the store when we receive a valid legal notice about it, and tell its author
when we can. Rights holders can use [REPORTING.md](REPORTING.md). A blocked extension is turned off in
the app; the entry gives the reason.

An author who thinks a block is wrong can answer through the same form, with their reasons.

## No guarantee

The sandbox limits what an extension can do, but it is not a guarantee. This policy can change; the
version in this repository is the one that applies.
